About

The short version of how I got here, and what I do best.

I started in global BPO customer service in the Philippines, learning how to handle hundreds of conversations a day under brand and SLA standards. From there I moved to Dubai, where I spent nearly three years at Merisant Company supporting B2B sales, trade marketing, and distributor relationships across the GCC region.

Since 2023, I have worked as a remote freelancer for DTC e-commerce, eco-conscious consumer goods, and B2B SaaS brands, owning end-to-end customer support and social media. Today I lead bilingual social strategy for Edunakho Group and Vertical Freediving in Barcelona while completing my master's in Digital Marketing.

My sweet spot is hybrid roles where customer experience depth and digital marketing curiosity both matter, and where I can keep growing the marketing side of my craft.
